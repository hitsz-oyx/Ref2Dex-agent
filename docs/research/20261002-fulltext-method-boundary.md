These are inferences about overlap, not claims of identical implementations.
AD-WM's latent visual representation can retain hand motion; it is inappropriate
to treat every object-effect null action as a counterexample to its full latent
action recovery. Our earlier null-object experiments therefore cannot be sold
as a blanket rejection of predictor-level inverse objectives.

The current learned-feature experiment is justified by an unresolved empirical
question: can short physical predictions improve a policy trained on actual
longer retention returns, under common fresh data and matched controls? It uses
no model reward and differs from our failed fixed-coefficient control variate.
Even a positive single-seed answer would be only a feasibility signal. A journal
contribution still requires a precise technical advance, independent Validation,
broader relevant physical settings, serious competing methods and a claim whose
scope matches the actual evidence. Giving this baseline a new acronym is not an
advance. No new general scientific claim is made here.
