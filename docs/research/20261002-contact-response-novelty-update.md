# Primary-source novelty update, 2 October 2026

This update constrains ideas; it does not validate our method or reproduce
external performance. Abstract-level primary-source inspection only.

- [WorldSimProbe, Co et al., v1, 10 August2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09298v1)
  defines action-realization and physically grounded environment-response
  requirements, tested through five controlled evaluation suites. Consequently
  generic action-to-hand-to-object factorization and simulator-faithfulness
  diagnostics cannot be presented as our original contribution. This is an
  inference about overlap, not a claim that implementations are identical.
- [Facet-0, Deng et al., v1, 1 September2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01596v1)
  combines action/wrench proposals, an action-wrench critic and RL adaptation.
  Predicting or valuing contact consequences, by itself, cannot establish our
  novelty. Differences in dexterous support identification must be demonstrated
  technically and through matched policy-training utility.

The prospective support-removal witness is useful experimental infrastructure,
not a novel grasp certificate. A future method needs a precise information
advantage over state-only, factual prediction, executable-command and compute
matched controls, and independent task benefit. A name for a representation
or another negative prediction pilot is insufficient. Full-paper comparison
and distinctive method design remain outstanding.
