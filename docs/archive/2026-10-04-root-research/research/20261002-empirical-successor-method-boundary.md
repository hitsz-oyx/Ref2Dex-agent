# Observed-support transitions: primary-source boundary

Consulted2October2026. The [Stanford institutional record for Kernel-Based
Reinforcement Learning](https://statistics.stanford.edu/technical-reports/kernel-based-reinforcement-learning)
identifies Ormoneit/Sen's1999technicalreport and its digital repository. The
repository redirect was unavailable to the browsing tool; no fulltext access
or theorem assumptions were verified there. Kernel-based RL is prior art;
our learned physical kernel/atomic probabilities are not claimed new by naming.

[ContactNets, CoRL2020, author PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.11193), introduction
and method sections, already addresses discontinuous contact behavior through
learned implicit distance/Jacobian representations and physically structured
training. Broad contact grounding, multimodality or realistic state support
are not sufficient novelty. Our empirical tuples provide neither its mechanics
constraints nor physically feasible conditional successors for arbitrary queries.

Own scientific distinction being tested: actual learned actors use expectation
of a nonlinear held-option value across observed physical tuples, with return-free
physical fitting, source-episode exclusion and strong action-off/direct-Q controls.
This is an implementation/causal comparison, not an established novel method.
Positive utility would still require direct mean-composition actor controls,
multi-training-seed Validation and specific prior-art review before a paper claim.
